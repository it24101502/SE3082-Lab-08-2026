MPI was used to parallelize the Monte Carlo estimation of π. A total of 10,000,000 random points were generated and divided equally among four MPI processes. Each process independently generated random coordinates and counted the number of points that fell inside the unit circle. The local counts were combined using MPI_Reduce() to obtain the total number of points inside the circle. Using the Monte Carlo formula, the estimated value of π was calculated as 3.1406736000, which is very close to the actual value of π (3.1415926535). This demonstrates the effectiveness of MPI for parallel numerical simulations.
